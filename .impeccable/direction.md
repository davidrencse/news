# Direction contract — Library of Babel

- **Decision:** The category standard, chosen by the user after a reroll. The user declined to name competitor products and asked for a simple, sleek, smooth reading library. Execute the familiar reading-library layout directly, without decorative novelty.
- **Audience and use:** One reader moves between saved Medium articles, a research preview, a personal notebook, and a local CVE catalog, then returns to long-form reading and notes.
- **First viewport:** A restrained source rail, a compact search/save bar, and a clear article index. The list identifies source, title, date, topic, and saved state. Opening an item produces a generous text measure with quiet, persistent reading controls and an optional notes margin.
- **Visitor path:** Source rail → topic or source shelf → searchable, scannable records → focused reader or CVE detail → notes/highlights → back to the shelf.
- **Signature interaction:** The reader and notes margin settle smoothly into a stable two-column reading spread; on narrow screens notes become a deliberate full-width panel and the navigation becomes a drawer.
- **Cross-surface reach:** Shared controls and source labels cover library, discovery, notebook, CVE catalog, and reader views. Keep article, research-preview, and CVE records visibly distinct. Do not imply a research provider exists.
- **World:** Conventional reading app. Bone and paper-white surfaces, near-black ink, subdued evergreen as the action color, warm neutral rules, serif reading type, clean sans controls, tabular figures for dates and CVE IDs. Flat surfaces with a single border vocabulary; no dashboard metrics, ornamental cards, or invented artwork.
- **Adaptation:** Desktop keeps the topic rail and broad reading measure. Tablets narrow the rail. Phones use the existing drawer, preserve touch targets, and keep search and reader controls reachable. Respect reduced-motion and forced colors.
- **Risk:** Simplicity can collapse source distinctions. Use explicit labels and stable metadata, not color alone, to identify the record type and state.
- **Scope:** Preserve every existing capability, interaction, and user-facing string. No data model or provider changes.
